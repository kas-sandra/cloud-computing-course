Mission Reflection

Mission 3 helped me gain a deeper understanding of the three major cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Before doing this activity, I knew that these platforms were used for cloud computing, but I did not have a clear understanding of how they differed from each other. Through my research, I discovered that each platform offers different features, services, benefits, and areas of specialization.

Among the three cloud platforms, Microsoft Azure impressed me the most because of the benefits it offers to students. Its free monthly resources and credits allow students to explore and practice cloud services without having to spend a lot of money. I believe this is especially helpful for students who are learning cloud computing and want to gain hands-on experience.

I also learned that AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud provide many similar services, including virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and identity management. However, each platform has its own strengths. AWS is recognized for its wide selection of cloud services, Azure is a good option for organizations that rely on Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud is particularly useful for Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

This activity also taught me that selecting a cloud provider should depend on the specific needs of an organization. Important factors such as budget, existing technology, performance, scalability, security, and business objectives should be considered before making a decision. I realized that there is no single cloud provider that is automatically the best choice for every organization.

Overall, Mission 3 improved my knowledge of cloud computing and gave me a better understanding of how cloud services are applied in real-world organizations. It also helped me enhance my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio by organizing my research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, and other laboratory activities. This experience has made me more confident in evaluating and comparing different cloud platforms.
